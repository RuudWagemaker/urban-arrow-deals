How the fair price works

  1. Comparables: the most similar listings (same variant, close in age, battery, motor, mileage) are found and each price is corrected for its differences. The fair price is their weighted median, and the range is the middle 50%.
  2. Listings that disappeared within a month (probably sold) count more. Listings still unsold after two months count less, because asking prices aren't sale prices.
  3. With too few comparables it falls back to the price model, and before enough data exists, to a list price × depreciation estimate (marked low confidence).
